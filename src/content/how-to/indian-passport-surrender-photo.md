## Do You Need a Photo to Surrender Your Indian Passport?

Yes. India doesn't allow dual citizenship, so when you become a British citizen you must surrender your Indian passport. The surrender application, which you need before you can apply for an OCI card, asks for recent passport-style photos.

In the UK, the High Commission of India in London and the consulates in Birmingham and Edinburgh handle surrender applications through an outsourced service provider. You fill in the online form, print it, attach your photos and submit it with your old Indian passport and proof of British citizenship.

A photo that's the wrong size, has a shadow or has a coloured background is one of the most common reasons an application gets sent back. This guide shows you how to get it right.

## Indian Passport Surrender Photo Requirements

Always check the current checklist from your service provider before you print, because the photo rules for Indian consular services in the UK have changed over the years and can differ by service. These are the typical requirements:

| Requirement | Typical rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Size | 35 × 45 mm (some checklists still ask for 2 × 2 inches / 51 × 51 mm) |
| Number of photos | Usually two identical photos |
| Background | Plain white, with no shadows or patterns |
| Age of photo | Taken recently, not reused from an old passport |
| Face | Full face, front view, eyes open, neutral expression |
| Head size | Face should fill most of the frame, from the top of the head to the chin |
| Glasses | Best removed to avoid glare and frames covering your eyes |
| Head covering | Only for religious reasons, with your full face visible |
| Finish | Colour, printed on good-quality photo paper, not a home printout on plain paper |

India's newer passport photo rules follow the ICAO standard used by most countries, which is why 35 × 45 mm is now the common size. If you want to understand what that standard covers, this [ICAO standard photo](https://icao-standard-photo.online/) resource explains the international rules for face position, lighting and background that passport authorities rely on.

## How to Take an Indian Passport Surrender Photo at Home in 6 Steps

1. **Check your service provider's photo checklist.** Open the current surrender checklist from the High Commission of India in London or its outsourced service provider. Note the photo size, the number of photos and whether they must be signed or pasted on the form.
2. **Stand against a plain white background.** Use a plain white wall or sheet with no pattern, texture or objects. Stand about half a metre away from it so you don't cast a shadow.
3. **Use soft, even daylight.** Face a window during the day so light falls evenly on both sides of your face. Turn off the flash and avoid overhead lights.
4. **Take the photo straight on.** Put your phone at eye level about one to one and a half metres away. Face the camera with your head upright, a neutral expression, mouth closed and both eyes open.
5. **Crop to the required size.** Crop the photo to the size on your checklist, usually 35 × 45 mm or 2 × 2 inches, with your full face, ears and the top of your shoulders in the frame.
6. **Print and check before you submit.** Print the photos on matte or semi-gloss photo paper at the exact size. Check them against the checklist before you take them to your appointment.

## 35 × 45 mm or 2 × 2 Inches: Which Size Do You Need?

For many years, Indian passport and consular services in the UK asked for a square 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) photo. Indian passport photos now generally follow the international 35 × 45 mm format, but surrender checklists aren't always updated at the same time.

- **If your checklist says 35 × 45 mm**, use a standard passport-size photo, the same size as a UK passport photo.
- **If your checklist says 2 × 2 inches**, use a square photo. Don't stretch a 35 × 45 mm photo to fit.
- **If it doesn't say**, contact your service provider before you print, rather than guessing.

Whichever size you need, the face and background rules are the same. A photo that meets the [ICAO compliant passport photo](https://icao-standard-photo.online/icao-compliant-passport-photo) rules for face size, centring and a plain background is the safest starting point, and you only need to change the crop.

## Can I Use the Same Photo for My Surrender Certificate and OCI Card?

Usually, but check the size first. You'll typically apply for your OCI card after you receive your surrender certificate. The OCI application also asks for a photo, often as a digital upload with its own pixel and file size limits, plus printed copies for your appointment.

If you take a sharp, evenly lit photo against a white background, you can crop the same picture for both applications. Just make sure it's still recent when you apply for the OCI card, as you shouldn't reuse a photo that's several months old. ## Common Mistakes With Indian Passport Surrender Photos

- **Using the wrong size.** A 35 × 45 mm photo isn't the same as a 2 × 2 inch photo. Check the checklist.
- **An off-white or grey background.** Indian services usually ask for plain white, not the light grey accepted for UK passports.
- **Shadows behind your head.** Step away from the wall and face the window.
- **Your head is too small in the frame.** Your face should fill most of the photo, not sit in the middle of a lot of background.
- **Glare on glasses.** Take them off unless you have a medical reason to keep them on.
- **Printing on plain paper.** Home printouts on office paper are often rejected. Use photo paper or a print shop.
- **Reusing your old Indian passport photo.** The photo must be recent and show how you look now.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size photo do I need to surrender my Indian passport in the UK?

Most applications now use a 35 × 45 mm passport-size photo, but some checklists still ask for a 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) square photo. Check the current checklist from the High Commission of India or its service provider before you print.

### How many photos do I need for an Indian passport surrender?

Usually two identical, recent photos. The exact number can change, so confirm it on your checklist.

### What background colour does an Indian passport surrender photo need?

Plain white, with no shadows, patterns or objects behind you.

### Can I take my Indian passport surrender photo at home?

Yes. Use a white wall, daylight from a window and a phone at eye level, then crop the photo to the right size and print it on photo paper. Our [Indian passport photo maker](/tool/indian-passport-photo-maker) can crop it and make the background white.

### Can I wear glasses in my Indian passport surrender photo?

It's best to remove them. Glasses can cause glare or cover your eyes, which can get your photo rejected.

### Do I need a new photo for my OCI application?

You can often use the same photo if it's still recent, but the OCI application has its own size and file rules, especially for the digital upload. Check them before you reuse a photo.

### Is the Indian surrender photo the same as a UK passport photo?

The size can be the same (35 × 45 mm), but the background usually differs. Indian services ask for plain white, while UK passport photos can be cream or light grey.
