# UK Passport Photo Size (mm, Inches and Head Size)

> **Quick answer:** A UK passport photo is 35 mm wide × 45 mm high (3.5 × 4.5 cm, or 1.38 × 1.77 inches). On a printed photo, your head must measure 29 to 34 mm from chin to crown. Digital photos for online applications must be at least 600 × 750 pixels.

## What size is a UK passport photo?

### Size in mm, cm and inches (table)

The official size is 35 mm wide and 45 mm high. This is the standard size used in UK photo booths.

| Unit | Width × height |
| :--- | :--- |
| Millimetres | 35 × 45 mm |
| Centimetres | 3.5 × 4.5 cm |
| Inches | 1.38 × 1.77 in |
| Pixels at 300 DPI (print) | 413 × 531 px |
| Pixels at 600 DPI (print) | 827 × 1063 px |
| Digital upload minimum | 600 × 750 px |

The photo is portrait. "45 × 35 mm" is the same photo with height listed first.

### Head size: chin to crown

On a printed photo, your head must measure between 29 mm and 34 mm. You measure from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head (the crown).

That means your head fills roughly two-thirds to three-quarters of the photo's height. The photo should be a close-up of your full head and upper shoulders.

### Why the size matters to the passport office

Your passport photo is used to identify you. A head that is too small or too large makes your face harder to check.

GOV.UK also says the photo must not be a cut-down version of a larger picture. If your photos do not meet the rules, your application will be delayed.

## Printed photo size vs digital photo size

### Printed: 35 x 45 mm

Printed photos are for paper applications. You need 2 identical photos, each exactly 35 × 45 mm, in colour on plain white photographic paper with no border.

| Printed rule | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Photo size | 35 mm wide × 45 mm high |
| Head size | 29 mm to 34 mm |
| Number | 2 identical prints |
| Background | Plain cream or light grey |

### Digital: minimum 600 x 750 pixels, 50 KB to 10 MB

Digital photos are for online applications. GOV.UK sets a minimum size rather than an exact one.

| Digital rule | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pixel size | At least 600 wide × 750 high |
| File size | 50 KB to 10 MB |
| Background | Plain and light-coloured |

If you take the photo on your own device during the application, do not crop it. GOV.UK crops it for you.

### Which one do you need?

If you apply online, you need a digital photo. You can use a code from a photo booth or shop, or take one during the application.

If you apply with a paper form, you need two printed photos. For all the other rules on background, pose and glasses, read our [full UK passport photo requirements](/how-to/uk-passport-photo-requirements).

## Passport photo size vs other common sizes

### 35x45mm vs 2x2 inch (US)

A US passport or visa photo is square: 2 × 2 inches, which is 51 × 51 mm. That is wider and taller than a UK photo.

You cannot use one for the other without re-cropping. See our [US visa photo requirements](/how-to/us-visa-photo-requirements) if you need the square size.

### UK vs Schengen vs Indian passport photo size

| Document | Common photo size |
| :--- | :--- |
| UK passport | 35 × 45 mm |
| Schengen visa | Commonly 35 × 45 mm |
| Indian passport and consular services | Commonly 35 × 45 mm; some checklists still ask for 2 × 2 in |
| US passport and visa | 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) |

The size can match, but other rules such as background colour often differ. Always check the official checklist for each document.

### "Passport size" in India vs the UK

In the UK, "passport size" means 35 × 45 mm. In India, the phrase is used more loosely, and some forms and checklists ask for 2 × 2 inch photos instead.

If a form only says "passport size photo", check which country's rules it follows before you print.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a UK passport photo 35x45mm?

Yes. A printed UK passport photo must be 35 mm wide and 45 mm high, which GOV.UK calls the standard size used in UK photo booths. Your head must measure 29 to 34 mm from chin to crown. Digital photos for online applications use a pixel minimum of 600 × 750 instead.

### What is the UK passport photo size in inches?

A UK passport photo is about 1.38 inches wide and 1.77 inches high. The official size is set in millimetres (35 × 45 mm), so always print using the millimetre size where possible. It is not the same as the 2 × 2 inch square photo used for US passports and visas.

### What size is a UK passport photo in pixels?

For online applications, GOV.UK asks for at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels high, with a file size of 50 KB to 10 MB. For printing at 35 × 45 mm, a 300 DPI file is 413 × 531 pixels and a 600 DPI file is 827 × 1063 pixels.

### Is passport size the same as stamp size?

No. A stamp-size photo is smaller than a passport photo and is not accepted for a UK passport. A UK passport photo must be exactly 35 × 45 mm with your head 29 to 34 mm tall. If a form asks for stamp size, follow that form's own measurements.

### Can I resize a photo myself?

Yes, as long as the original is sharp and shows your full head and upper shoulders. Crop to a 35 × 45 mm shape and keep your head 29 to 34 mm tall when printed. Do not use filters or other edits. A free tool like ours does the maths for you.
