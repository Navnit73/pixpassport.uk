# DVLA Photo: How to Take and Change Your Driving Licence Photo

## Can You Take Your Own DVLA Photo at Home?

Yes. You can take your own driving licence photo with a smartphone, a plain wall and some daylight. You do not need a photo booth. The DVLA asks for a recent, passport-style photo, so a clear, well-lit image with a plain background gives you the best chance of acceptance.

This guide shows you how to take the photo, what rules to follow and how to apply.

## How to Take a Driving Licence Photo at Home in 6 Steps

1. **Check that you need a new photo.** Renew your photocard licence every 10 years. Renew sooner if you want a new photo, because you can only change your photo by renewing your licence.
2. **Set up a plain background and soft light.** Stand in front of a plain, light-coloured wall and face a window. Turn off the flash and any overhead lights so no shadows fall on your face or the wall.
3. **Position your phone.** Rest your phone at eye level on a tripod or stack of books, or ask someone to take the photo from about one to one and a half metres away.
4. **Pose with a neutral expression.** Face the camera straight on with your head upright, your mouth closed and your eyes open. Take off your glasses and keep your hair away from your face.
5. **Take several photos and choose the best.** Take five or six shots with the rear camera. Switch off filters and beauty mode, then pick the sharpest, most even photo.
6. **Renew online or by post.** Apply online on GOV.UK and use your passport photo or upload your new one. Alternatively, send a printed photo with a D1 form by post.

### What You Need Before You Start

- A smartphone with a working rear camera
- A plain, light wall or a pale sheet
- A window with soft daylight
- Your current driving licence and, for online renewals, your National Insurance number and addresses from the last three years
- About 15 minutes

## DVLA Photo Requirements

The DVLA expects the same standard as a UK passport photo. Check the latest wording on GOV.UK before you apply, because the rules can change.

| Requirement    | What to do                                                                       |
| :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Background     | Use a plain, light-coloured wall with no pattern or texture                      |
| Lighting       | Light your face evenly, with no shadows on your face or behind you               |
| Expression     | Keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed                                 |
| Pose           | Face the camera and keep your head straight                                      |
| Glasses        | Take them off, so glare or frames do not hide your eyes                          |
| Head coverings | Wear one only for religious or medical reasons, and keep your face fully visible |
| Quality        | Submit a sharp, in-focus colour photo with no filters or red-eye                 |
| Age of photo   | Use a photo taken recently, ideally within the last month                        |

## How to Change or Renew the Photo on Your Driving Licence

You cannot simply swap your photo. You must renew your full or provisional licence to get a new one with an updated photo.

### Renew Your Photo Online

Most people can renew online on GOV.UK, and it costs £14. You must live in Great Britain and not be disqualified from driving. The online service lets you use your passport photo or upload a new one. You do not need to get your photo signed when you apply online.

#### When You Cannot Renew Online

You cannot use the online service if your name or title has changed since you received your licence, or if you hold a 5-year bus or lorry licence. Apply by post instead.

### Renew Your Photo by Post

Complete a D1 form, which you can get from a Post Office or order from the DVLA, and send it with:

- Your current photocard licence
- A recent, printed passport-style photo
- The fee, which is £17 by post at the time of writing

The DVLA says a new licence should arrive within three weeks. You can keep driving while you wait.

### Change a Photo on a Provisional Licence

You can update the photo on a provisional licence in the same way. Renew it online or by post, and follow the same photo rules.

### Changing Your Address or Name

Changing your address does not by itself need a new photo, and you can do it separately. A name change cannot go through the online photo service. Apply by post with the supporting documents, and renew your photo in the same application.

## Fees and Timings at a Glance

| Method            | Cost | Typical wait                             |
| :---------------- | :--- | :--------------------------------------- |
| Online            | £14  | Within about a week, depending on demand |
| By post (D1 form) | £17  | Up to three weeks                        |

Drivers aged 70 or over, and people with a medical short-period licence, pay nothing. Always check the current fee on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/renew-photo-driving-licence) before you pay.

## Common DVLA Photo Mistakes

- **Shadows on your face or behind your head.** Step away from the wall and face the window.
- **A patterned or dark background.** Use a plain, light wall.
- **Glasses, hats or hair across the eyes.** Remove them or move them aside.
- **Filters and edits.** Never retouch or smooth the photo.
- **An old or blurred photo.** Take a new one and hold the phone steady.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I take a driving licence photo at home?

Stand facing a window in front of a plain, light wall. Rest your phone at eye level, keep a neutral expression and take several photos. Choose the sharpest one and upload it with your renewal.

### How do I change the photo on my driving licence?

Renew your licence on GOV.UK. The service lets you use your passport photo or upload a new one, and it costs £14 online.

### Can I use my passport photo for my DVLA licence?

Yes. When you renew online, the service lets you use your passport photo or upload a new one.

### How often must I renew my driving licence photo?

Renew your photocard licence every 10 years. The DVLA sends you a reminder before it expires.

### How much does a DVLA photo renewal cost?

It costs £14 online and £17 by post. You pay nothing if you are 70 or over or hold a medical short-period licence.

### Do I need someone to sign my photo?

You do not need a signature when you renew online. If you apply by post, follow the instructions that come with the D1 form.

### Can I change the photo on my provisional licence?

Yes. Renew your provisional licence in the same way, online or by post.

### What if my name has changed?

You cannot renew online. Apply by post with your supporting documents, and update your photo in the same application.

### Can I wear glasses in my driving licence photo?

No. Take them off so your eyes are clearly visible with no glare.
