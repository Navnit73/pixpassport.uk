# Change Photo on Driving Licence (DVLA): Steps, Fee and Free Photo Check

> **Quick answer:** You cannot swap the photo on its own. To change the photo on your driving licence, you **renew your licence with a new photo**. It costs **£14 online**, **£17 by post** (D1 form) or **£21.50 at a Post Office**. It is free if you are 70 or over. Online applications usually arrive **within a week**.
>

## How to Change the Photo on Your Driving Licence in 6 Steps

Most people can update their driving licence photo online in about 15 minutes. Here is the full process:

1. **Check you can change your photo online.** You can apply online if you live in Great Britain, are not disqualified from driving, and your name, title and signature have not changed. Otherwise apply by post or at a Post Office.
2. **Get your new photo ready.** Take a recent passport-style photo against a plain light wall, or choose to reuse your digital UK passport photo during the application.
3. **Gather your details.** Have your current driving licence, your addresses for the last 3 years and, if you have one, your National Insurance number and UK passport number.
4. **Apply on GOV.UK.** Use the GOV.UK change-the-photo service, upload your new photo or choose your passport photo, and pay the £14 fee by card.
5. **Wait for your new licence.** An online application should arrive within a week. You can keep driving while you wait.
6. **Send back your old licence.** When your new licence arrives, post your old photocard to the DVLA. The address is shown at the end of your application.

## Update Your Driving Licence Photo Online (GOV.UK)

The online service is the quickest way to update your driving licence photo. It works for full and provisional photocard licences.

### Who Can Change Their Photo Online

You can apply online if you:

- live in England, Scotland or Wales
- are not disqualified from driving
- have not changed your name, title or signature since your current licence was issued

### Can You Use Your Passport Photo?

Yes. If you have a valid UK passport, the GOV.UK service can use your digital passport photo, so you do not need a new one. If your passport photo is old or no longer looks like you, upload a new photo instead.

### What Photo to Upload

Upload a recent colour photo with a plain cream or light grey background, a neutral expression and no glasses. It should be at least 600 × 750 pixels. Our [driving licence photo tool](/tool/uk-driving-licence-photo) crops and sizes your photo to this standard. You can also run any photo through the free [passport photo checker](/tool/passport-photo-checker-uk) before you upload.

### When You Cannot Change Your Photo Online

You must apply by post or at a Post Office if:

- your name or title has changed
- your signature has changed
- you hold a 5-year bus or lorry licence

## DVLA Change Photo by Post (D1 Form)

1. Get a **D1 application pack** from a Post Office or order one from the DVLA.
2. Fill in the form and tick the box to renew with a new photo.
3. Attach a **recent printed passport-style photo** (35 × 45 mm) where the form shows.
4. Include your **current photocard licence** and a cheque or postal order for **£17**.
5. Post it to the address on the form.

Your new licence should arrive within 3 weeks. To print the photo yourself, use our [passport photo print template generator](/passport-photo-print-template-generator) or follow our guide to [printing passport photos at home](/print-passport-photos-at-home).

## Change Your Driving Licence Photo at the Post Office

Selected Post Office branches offer a photocard renewal service. Staff take your photo and signature digitally at the counter, so you do not need to bring a printed photo. It costs **£21.50**, which includes the Post Office service charge. Bring your reminder letter or your current photocard licence. Use the Post Office branch finder to check which branches offer the service before you go.

## Change the Photo on a Provisional Licence

You update a provisional licence photo in exactly the same way as a full licence. Renew online for £14, by post for £17 or at a Post Office. The photo rules are the same. If you are about to pass your test, you can wait, because you will get a new full licence with your existing photo.

## Change Your Photo and Address at the Same Time

Changing your address is free and does not need a new photo. If you want both, apply to change your photo and enter your new address in the same application, so you get one new licence. If your licence is valid for less than 2 years, GOV.UK lets you update your photo when you change your address.

## Northern Ireland: Change Your Licence Photo With the DVA

Northern Ireland licences come from the **Driver and Vehicle Agency (DVA)**, not the DVLA. Apply through the DVA service on [nidirect](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/information-and-services/motoring/driver-licensing). The DVA sets its own fees and photo instructions, so check them before you apply.

## DVLA Photo Requirements for Your New Photo

The DVLA uses the same standard as a UK passport photo:

| Requirement    | What the DVLA expects                                                   |
| :------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size           | 35 mm wide × 45 mm high (digital: at least 600 × 750 px)                |
| Head size      | 29 mm to 34 mm from chin to crown                                       |
| Background     | Plain cream or light grey, with no pattern or shadow                    |
| Expression     | Neutral, mouth closed, eyes open and looking at the camera              |
| Glasses        | Take them off                                                           |
| Head coverings | Only for religious or medical reasons, with your full face visible      |
| Quality        | Sharp colour photo with no filters, red-eye or editing                  |
| Age of photo   | Taken recently, so it looks like you now                                |

For the full rules, read our [driving licence photo requirements](/how-to/driving-licence-photo-requirements-uk) guide.

### Take Your New Licence Photo at Home

- Stand about half a metre in front of a plain, light wall and face a window.
- Ask someone to hold the phone at eye level, one to one and a half metres away.
- Turn off the flash, filters and beauty mode.
- Take five or six shots and keep the sharpest one.

## Common Reasons a DVLA Photo Change Is Delayed

- **Shadows on your face or behind your head.** Step away from the wall and face the window.
- **A patterned, white or dark background.** Use a plain cream or light grey wall.
- **Glasses, hats or hair across your eyes.** Remove them or move them aside.
- **Filters or retouching.** Upload the original photo.
- **A blurred or low-resolution photo.** Hold the phone steady and use the rear camera.
- **A name or signature change.** These cannot go through the online service. Apply by post.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I change the photo on my driving licence?

Renew your licence with a new photo on GOV.UK. Upload a new photo or reuse your passport photo, and pay £14. You can also apply by post with a D1 form for £17, or at a Post Office for £21.50.

### Can I change my driving licence photo without renewing?

No. The only way to update the photo is to renew your licence. Your new licence is valid for 10 years from the date it is issued.

### How much does it cost to change the photo on a driving licence?

It costs £14 online, £17 by post or £21.50 at a Post Office. It is free if you are 70 or over or hold a medical short-period licence.

### How long does a DVLA photo change take?

Online applications should arrive within a week. Postal and Post Office applications take up to 3 weeks, or longer if the DVLA needs to check your details.

### Can I use my passport photo for my driving licence?

Yes. If you have a valid UK passport, the online service can use your digital passport photo.

### Can I drive while waiting for my new licence?

Yes. You can keep driving while the DVLA processes your application, as long as you meet the usual conditions to drive.

### Do I need to send my old licence back?

Yes. If you apply online, post your old photocard to the DVLA after your new licence arrives. If you apply by post, send it with your D1 form.

### Do I need someone to sign my photo?

No, not when you apply online. If you apply by post, follow the instructions in the D1 pack.

### Can I change the photo on my provisional licence?

Yes. Use the same online, postal or Post Office service. The fee and photo rules are the same as for a full licence.

### How often can I change my driving licence photo?

As often as you like, but you pay the fee each time. You must renew your photocard licence every 10 years, and the DVLA sends a reminder before it expires.

### What if my name has changed?

You cannot use the online service. Apply by post with a D1 form and your supporting documents, and update your photo in the same application.

### Can I wear glasses in my driving licence photo?

No. Take them off so your eyes are clearly visible with no glare or frames in the way.
