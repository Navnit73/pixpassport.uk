## Official UK Passport Photo Rules (HMPO Guidelines)

When applying for or renewing a British passport online at [gov.uk/apply-renew-passport](https://www.gov.uk/apply-renew-passport), your digital photo must comply with strict biometric regulations set by His Majesty's Passport Office (HMPO):

### Key UK Passport Photo Specifications
- **Dimensions**: Exactly 35 mm wide by 45 mm high (or at least 600 × 750 pixels for digital uploads).
- **Head Size**: From the crown of your head to the bottom of your chin must measure between **29 mm and 34 mm** (approx 70% to 80% of total height).
- **Background**: Plain cream, light grey, or off-white. No objects, patterns, shadows, or wall textures.
- **Pose & Expression**: Face straight into the camera, eyes open and clearly visible, mouth closed with a neutral expression (no smiling or frowning).
- **Eyeglasses**: Prescribed glasses are allowed only if there is zero flash glare, no reflection, and frames do not obscure your eyes. Sunglasses are prohibited.

## What to Do vs What to Avoid

| Required Checklist ✅ | Common Rejection Reasons ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Look directly at the camera with a neutral expression | Smiling, laughing, or frowning |
| Plain, shadowless light background | Dark, patterned, or textured home background |
| Both eyes clearly visible and open | Hair covering eyes or thick spectacle frames |
| High resolution (300+ DPI, clear focus) | Blurry, pixelated, or compressed social media photos |
| Full head, neck, and shoulders in frame | Cropping top of hair or chin |

## How to Submit Your UK Passport Photo

### 1. Online Passport Renewal (Gov.uk)
1. Download your verified single digital photo (`.jpg`) generated above.
2. Visit the official UK government portal: [gov.uk/apply-renew-passport](https://www.gov.uk/apply-renew-passport).
3. Upload the photo directly into the HMPO digital photo tool — it will validate automatically.

### 2. Paper Passport Application Form (Post Office Check & Send)
