> **Quick answer:** A printed UK passport photo must be **35 mm wide × 45 mm high**, with your head **29–34 mm** from chin to crown, on a **plain cream or light grey** background. A digital photo must be at least **600 × 750 pixels** and **50KB to 10MB**, on a plain light-coloured background. Both must be in colour, unedited and **taken in the last month**.

If your photo breaks one of the HM Passport Office (HMPO) requirements, your application is delayed until you send a new one. This page lists every requirement, explains what each one means in practice, and lets you make and check a compliant photo in your browser.

## UK Passport Photo Size Requirements

### Printed photo size in mm, cm and inches

| Unit | UK passport photo size |
| :--- | :--- |
| Millimetres | 35 × 45 mm |
| Centimetres | 3.5 × 4.5 cm |
| Inches | About 1.38 × 1.77 in |
| Aspect ratio | 7:9 (portrait) |

This is the standard UK photo booth size. The photo must not be a cut-down version of a larger picture. Need a visual guide? See our [35x45 mm photo size diagram](/tool/photo-size-35x45mm).

### Head size: 29 mm to 34 mm

On a printed photo, the image of your head, from the bottom of your chin to the crown of your head, must be **between 29 mm and 34 mm high**. A head that is too small or too large is one of the most common reasons booth-free photos fail.

### Digital photo size in pixels

A digital photo must be **at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall**, and the file must be **between 50KB and 10MB**. Upload the original file from your phone, not a screenshot or a copy compressed by a messaging app.

If you take the photo on your own device, **do not crop it**. Include your head, shoulders and upper body, because the GOV.UK service crops it for you.

## UK Passport Photo Background Requirements

### Printed photos: plain cream or light grey

Printed photos need a **plain cream or light grey** background in clear contrast to you, with no pattern, texture, objects or other people.

### Digital photos: plain and light-coloured

Digital photos need a **plain, light-coloured** background. The same rule on contrast applies: your face, hair and shoulders must stand out clearly.

### Can I use a white wall?

A plain white wall usually counts as light-coloured for a digital photo. Bright white can wash out pale skin and blond hair, though, so a light grey or cream surface is the safer choice. For printed photos, the rule says cream or light grey. Our editor can replace a cluttered background with a plain light one, but it cannot remove shadows from your face.

### No shadows on your face or behind you

There must be no shadows on your face or on the wall behind you. Face a window for even daylight and stand about half a metre away from the wall.

## Expression and Pose Requirements

### Can I smile in a UK passport photo?

No. You must have a **plain expression with your mouth closed**. A small smile can still delay your application. Children under 6 are the exception.

### Head position and eyes

- Face forwards and look straight at the camera.
- Keep your head upright, not tilted up, down or to the side.
- Keep both eyes open and visible, with no hair in front of them.
- Make sure there is no red-eye. Daylight instead of flash avoids it.

## Glasses Requirements for UK Passport Photos

GOV.UK says **do not wear glasses unless you have to**. If you must wear them:

- They cannot be sunglasses or tinted glasses.
- The frames must not cover your eyes.
- There must be no glare, reflection or shadow on the lenses.

Taking them off removes the risk altogether.

## Hair, Hats and Religious Head Coverings

You must not wear a head covering unless it is for **religious or medical reasons**. If you wear one, such as a hijab or turban, your full face must be visible from chin to forehead, and the covering must not cast shadows on your face. Our [hijab passport photo guide](/tool/hijab-passport-photo) shows how to position it.

Keep hair away from your eyes and eyebrows. Nothing can cover your face, and there must be no other objects in the photo, so remove earphones and hair accessories that hide your face.

## Photo Quality and Editing Requirements

- **Colour:** the photo must be in colour.
- **Focus:** sharp, clear and in focus, with even lighting.
- **No editing:** it must not be altered by computer software. No beauty filters, skin smoothing or colour changes.
- **Recent:** taken in the last month and a good likeness of you now.
- **Alone:** you must be the only person in the photo.

## UK Passport Photo Requirements for Children and Babies

Children use the same size and background rules, but some pose rules are relaxed:

- **Under 6:** they do not have to look straight at the camera or have a plain expression.
- **Under 1:** they do not need their eyes open. Lay them on a plain light-coloured sheet and photograph them from above.
- **All children:** they must be alone in the photo, with no toys or dummies. You can support a baby's head, but your hand must not be visible.

## Digital Photo, Photo Code or Printed Photos: Which Do You Need?

| Route | You need | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Apply online, own photo | A digital photo that meets the digital requirements | Fast, low-cost renewals and first applications |
| Apply online, photo code | A code from a booth or shop, entered during your application | Extra certainty, at a higher price |
| Apply by post | 2 identical printed photos, 35 × 45 mm | Paper applications |

### Applying online on GOV.UK

Upload a photo you have taken yourself, or enter a photo code. GOV.UK says photos from a booth or shop are more likely to be approved than photos taken on your own device, so check a home photo carefully before you submit. Our [digital passport photo](/tool/digital-passport-photo) page explains both routes.

### Applying by post

Send 2 identical photos, printed to a professional standard on plain white photographic paper, with no border, creases or marks. Do not attach them to the form. First applications, lost or stolen replacements, renewals for children aged 11 or under and renewals where your appearance has changed also need a countersignatory. They write "I certify that this is a true likeness of [name]" on the back of one photo, then sign and date it.

### Printing your photos

Ask a photo lab or shop to print your sheet at 100% scale with no "fit to page", so each photo stays exactly 35 × 45 mm. Prefer your own printer? Follow our guide to [printing passport photos at home](/print-passport-photos-at-home).

## Accepted vs Rejected UK Passport Photos

| Accepted ✅ | Rejected ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Plain expression, mouth closed | Smiling, laughing or frowning |
| Plain cream or light grey background | Dark, patterned or shadowed background |
| Both eyes open and clearly visible | Hair across the eyes, or heavy frames |
| Sharp, colour image | Blurry, pixelated or filtered picture |
| Full head, neck and shoulders in frame | Cropped chin or crown, or a tight close-up |
| Taken in the last month | Old photo, or one edited to smooth skin |
| Head, shoulders and upper body (online upload) | A tightly cropped face uploaded to GOV.UK |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the UK passport photo requirements?

Your photo must be 35 × 45 mm (printed) or at least 600 × 750 px and 50KB to 10MB (digital). It must show you alone, facing forwards with a plain expression, on a plain light background with no shadows, in colour, unedited and taken in the last month.

### What size is a UK passport photo?

A printed UK passport photo measures 45 mm high by 35 mm wide (4.5 × 3.5 cm), with your head 29–34 mm from chin to crown. A digital photo must be at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall.

### What background do I need for a UK passport photo?

Use a plain cream or light grey background for printed photos, and a plain light-coloured background for digital photos. There must be no patterns, objects or shadows.

### Can I smile in my UK passport photo?

No. Keep a plain expression with your mouth closed and your eyes open. Children under 6 do not need a plain expression.

### Can I wear glasses in my UK passport photo?

Only if you have to. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are not allowed, and the frames, glare or reflections must not cover your eyes.

### Can I wear a hijab or turban in my passport photo?

Yes, if you wear it for religious reasons. Your full face must be visible from chin to forehead, with no shadows on it.

### How recent must my passport photo be?

It must have been taken in the last month and look like you now. You need a new photo every time you apply for a new passport.

### Can I take a UK passport photo with my phone?

Yes. Ask someone to take it at eye level, about 1 to 1.5 metres away, in front of a plain light wall facing a window. Then check it before you upload.
