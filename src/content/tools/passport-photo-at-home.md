## Take Your Own Passport Photo at Home

You can take a passport photo at home with nothing more than a phone and a plain wall. Take the picture, upload it to our tool, and we size it, replace the background and check your head against the official range. Then print the photo yourself, or download the file for an online application.

Doing it at home saves travel and queues, and you can retake the picture as often as you like. This guide shows you how to set up, shoot and print a photo that meets the rules.

## How to Take a Passport Photo at Home in 5 Steps

1. **Set up your space.** Choose a plain wall and a well-lit spot, and follow the setup checklist below.
2. **Take the picture.** Ask someone to hold the phone at eye level and shoot a head-and-shoulders portrait.
3. **Upload your photo.** Choose your country, then upload the picture to the tool.
4. **Review the checks.** The tool crops the photo, replaces the background and tests your head size.
5. **Print or download.** Save a digital file, or print a sheet with several copies.

The whole process takes about 15 minutes, including setup.

## What You Need

You already own most of the equipment.

- **A smartphone or camera:** Any recent phone takes a good enough picture.
- **A plain wall:** A light, matt wall with no pattern works best.
- **A helper:** Someone else can hold the camera at eye level, which avoids selfie distortion.
- **Soft daylight:** A large window gives even light on your face.
- **A printer and photo paper:** You need these only if your application asks for prints.
- **Scissors or a paper trimmer:** These give clean, exact edges.

## Set Up Your Home Photo Studio

Good lighting and a clean background make the biggest difference.

- **Face a window.** Stand so that daylight falls evenly on your face, with no shadows.
- **Avoid direct sun.** Harsh light creates shadows and squinting.
- **Stand in front of a plain wall.** Keep patterns, doors, frames and furniture out of the shot.
- **Stand about one metre from the wall.** This reduces shadows behind your head.
- **Keep the camera at eye level.** Have your helper stand about one metre from you.
- **Use the rear camera.** Selfie lenses distort your face.

## Tips for Taking Your Own ID Photo

- **Face the camera.** Look straight at the lens with your head level and your shoulders square.
- **Keep a neutral expression.** Close your mouth and keep your eyes open.
- **Show your full face.** Move hair away from your eyes and eyebrows.
- **Remove your glasses.** Many countries no longer accept them, except for documented medical reasons.
- **Wear a plain top.** Choose a colour that contrasts with your background.
- **Leave space around your head.** The tool needs room to crop correctly.
- **Take several shots.** Choose the sharpest, most natural picture.

## How to Print Your Passport Photo at Home

Printing is where many DIY photos go wrong. Follow these steps for a clean result.

1. **Download the print sheet.** Choose the 6 × 4 inch (10 × 15 cm) layout from the tool.
2. **Load photo paper.** Use glossy or matt photo paper, not plain office paper.
3. **Choose the highest quality setting.** Select "photo" or "best" in your printer settings.
4. **Print at 100% scale.** Turn off "fit to page" or "shrink to fit", or the size will change.
5. **Check the print.** Measure one photo with a ruler to confirm the exact size.
6. **Cut along the guides.** Use a trimmer or a sharp pair of scissors for straight edges.

### Print Settings Checklist

| Setting | Choose |
| :--- | :--- |
| Paper | Photo paper, glossy or matt |
| Quality | Highest or "photo" |
| Scale | 100%, with no fit to page |
| Borders | None |
| Colour | Full colour, with no filters |
| Resolution | 300 DPI or higher |

If your printer struggles, save the print sheet to your phone and take it to a pharmacy or supermarket kiosk. Ask for a 6 × 4 inch print with no cropping.

## Digital or Printed Photo?

Some applications need only a digital file, and others need prints.

| | Digital photo | Printed photo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Best for | Online applications and portals | Paper forms and in-person visits |
| Output | JPEG at the required pixel size | Prints at the exact millimetre size |
| Checking | Portals often check it automatically | You check it yourself |
| Printing | None needed | Photo paper at 100% scale |

Always check your application's instructions, because some forms ask for a countersignature on the back of one photo.

## Passport Photos for Children and Babies

Children need the same size and background, but many countries relax some rules for young ones. For a baby, lay them on a plain, light sheet and photograph them from above. No one else may appear in the frame. Check your country's guidance for children before you apply.
