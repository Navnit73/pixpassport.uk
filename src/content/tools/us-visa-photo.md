## Official US Visa & Passport Photo Requirements

When submitting an application for a United States visa (DS-160, DS-260) or US passport renewal, your photo must adhere to mandatory specifications from the U.S. Department of State:

### Key US Photo Specifications
- **Dimensions**: Exactly 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), or minimum 600 × 600 pixels (1:1 square aspect ratio).
- **Head Size**: Between 1 inch and 1 3/8 inches (25 mm to 35 mm) from bottom of chin to top of head (50% to 69% of total height).
- **Background**: Plain white or off-white with zero shadows or objects.
- **Eyeglasses**: **Strictly prohibited** in US visa and passport photos since 2016.
- **Expression**: Neutral facial expression or natural, unforced smile with both eyes open and facing straight at the lens.

## What to Do vs What to Avoid

| Required Checklist ✅ | Common Rejection Reasons ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Square 1:1 format (600×600 px or 2×2″) | Rectangular crop or stretched photo |
| No eyeglasses of any kind | Wearing glasses, tinted lenses, or sunglasses |
| Plain white or off-white background | Off-white with shadows or textured wallpaper |
| Direct front-facing eye level | Head tilted up, down, or turned sideways |
| Both ears/facial contours clearly visible | Heavy hair or scarves hiding facial boundary |
