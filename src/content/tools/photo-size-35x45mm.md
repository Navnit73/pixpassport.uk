## 35×45 mm Photo Requirements That Apply Almost Everywhere

Most authorities that use this size follow the ICAO principles. Get these right, and you avoid most rejections.

### Pose and Expression

- **Face the camera.** Look straight at the lens with your head level.
- **Keep a neutral expression.** Close your mouth and keep your eyes open.
- **Show your full face.** Move hair away from your eyes, and keep your face uncovered from chin to forehead.

### Background and Lighting

- **Use a plain background.** Check whether your country wants white, cream or light grey.
- **Avoid shadows.** Light your face and the wall evenly.
- **Keep the frame clear.** Other people, objects and patterns cause rejections.

### Glasses, Headwear and Editing

- **Remove your glasses.** Many countries no longer accept them, except for documented medical reasons.
- **Wear headwear only for religious or medical reasons.** Show your full face.
- **Do not retouch the photo.** Filters, smoothing and heavy editing lead to rejection.

## How to Print a 35x45 mm Photo

Printing is where many photos go wrong. Follow these steps for an exact size.

1. **Download the print sheet.** Choose the 6 × 4 inch (10 × 15 cm) layout.
2. **Load photo paper.** Use glossy or matt photo paper, not plain office paper.
3. **Choose the highest quality setting.** Select "photo" or "best" in your printer settings.
4. **Print at 100% scale.** Turn off "fit to page", or the size will change.
5. **Measure the result.** Check one photo with a ruler for exactly 35 × 45 mm.
6. **Cut along the guides.** Use a trimmer or sharp scissors for straight edges.
