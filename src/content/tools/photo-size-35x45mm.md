## 35x45 mm Photo Rules That Apply Almost Everywhere

Most authorities that use this size follow the [ICAO photo standards](/tool/icao-standards-photo-online-free). Get these right, and you avoid most rejections.

### Pose and expression

- **Face the camera.** Look straight at the lens with your head level.
- **Keep a neutral expression.** Close your mouth and keep your eyes open.
- **Show your full face.** Move hair away from your eyes.

### Background and lighting

- **Use a plain background.** Check whether your document wants white, cream or light grey.
- **Avoid shadows.** Light your face and the wall evenly.
- **Keep the frame clear.** Other people, objects and patterns cause rejections.

### Glasses, headwear and editing

- **Remove your glasses** where you can. Many countries no longer accept them.
- **Wear headwear only for religious or medical reasons**, with your full face visible. See our [hijab passport photo guide](/tool/hijab-passport-photo).
- **Do not retouch the photo.** Filters and smoothing lead to rejection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a 35 by 45 mm photo?

It is a portrait photo 35 mm wide and 45 mm high (3.5 × 4.5 cm). It is the standard passport photo size in the UK, Ireland, the EU and many other countries.

### What is 35x45 mm in cm and inches?

It is 3.5 × 4.5 cm, or about 1.38 × 1.77 inches.

### How many pixels is a 35x45 mm photo?

About 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI, or 827 × 1063 pixels at 600 DPI. The UK digital passport service asks for at least 600 × 750 pixels.

### How big should the head be in a 35x45 mm photo?

For a UK passport, your head must be 29–34 mm from chin to crown. Other countries use slightly different ranges.

### Can I use a 35x45 mm photo for a visa?
