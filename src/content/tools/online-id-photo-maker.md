## ID Photo Requirements That Apply Almost Everywhere

Each authority sets its own rules, but most follow the same ICAO principles. Get these right, and you avoid most rejections.

### Pose and Expression

- **Face the camera.** Look straight at the lens with your head level.
- **Keep a neutral expression.** Close your mouth and keep your eyes open.
- **Show your full face.** Move hair away from your eyes, and keep your face uncovered from chin to forehead.

### Background and Lighting

- **Use a plain background.** Most authorities ask for white, cream or light grey.
- **Avoid shadows.** Light your face and the wall evenly.
- **Keep the frame clear.** Other people, objects and patterns cause rejections.

### Glasses, Headwear and Editing

- **Remove your glasses.** Many authorities no longer accept them, except for documented medical reasons.
- **Wear headwear only for religious or medical reasons.** Show your full face.
- **Do not retouch the photo.** Filters, smoothing and heavy editing lead to rejection.

## Good vs Poor Source Pictures

The maker can crop, resize and clean up a photo, but it cannot rescue a poor one. Check your picture before you upload it.

| Good source picture ✅ | Poor source picture ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sharp, well-lit and in colour | Blurry, dark or grainy |
| Face looking straight at the camera | Head tilted, turned or looking away |
| Neutral expression, eyes open | Smiling widely, blinking or squinting |
| Head and shoulders with space around them | Tight crop or a full-body shot |
| Recent and natural | Old, filtered or heavily edited |
| Taken without glasses | Glare on lenses or sunglasses |

## How to Take a Better Picture

If your existing image does not pass the checklist, take a new one. A clear picture gives the cleanest result.

- **Ask someone to help.** Selfies distort your face, so have a friend hold the camera at eye level.
- **Face a window.** Soft daylight removes shadows from your face.
- **Stand about one metre from the camera.** Keep the lens level with your eyes.
- **Use a plain wall.** A simple backdrop gives a sharper background cut-out.
- **Leave space around your head.** The tool needs room to crop correctly.
- **Look straight ahead.** Relax your face and keep your head level.
