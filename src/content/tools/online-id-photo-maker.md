## Make an ID Card Photo Online

Most UK ID cards ask for the same thing: a recent, **passport-style** colour photo of your head and shoulders on a plain, light background. Our ID photo maker turns a phone picture into exactly that. It crops your face, sets the head size, replaces the background and gives you a digital file to upload, plus a 6×4″ sheet if you need a printed copy.

## ID Photo Requirements for UK ID Cards

### Size and File Format

- **Shape:** most UK ID cards use a portrait, passport-style frame (35 × 45 mm, ratio 7:9). A few staff and student portals use a square crop, which you can make from the same photo.
- **File:** JPEG is accepted almost everywhere. Upload limits are often a few megabytes.
- **Resolution:** a file of at least 600 × 750 pixels is sharp enough for any card printer.

### What the Photo Must Show

- **Your head and the top of your shoulders,** centred and facing the camera
- **A plain, light background** with no patterns, objects or other people
- **A neutral expression,** mouth closed and eyes open
- **No sunglasses or tinted lenses,** and no glare on clear glasses
- **No hat or head covering,** unless worn for religious or medical reasons, with your face fully visible
- **A recent photo** that looks like you now

### What Gets ID Photos Rejected

| Problem ❌ | Fix ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cropped holiday photo or group shot | Take a fresh, front-facing photo |
| Busy or dark background | Stand in front of a plain wall; the tool also replaces it |
| Shadows across your face | Face a window in soft daylight |
| Selfie distortion | Ask someone to take it from about a metre away |
| Filters or beauty mode | Turn them off; ID photos must be unedited |
| Too small or blurry | Use your phone's rear camera |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make an ID photo online?

Upload a front-facing photo, choose the document type, and the tool crops it, replaces the background and checks your head size. Preview it free, then download the file.

### What size is an ID card photo in the UK?

Most UK ID cards use a passport-style 35 × 45 mm portrait photo. Some staff and student portals ask for a square image instead. Check your issuer's instructions.

### Can I use my passport photo for an ID card?

Usually, yes, as long as it is recent. A passport-style photo meets the rules for most UK staff, student, CSCS and proof-of-age cards.

### What photo do I need for a CSCS card?

CSCS photos are usually taken at the test centre. If you are asked to upload one, use a recent passport-style photo on a plain, light background.

### What photo do I need for a CitizenCard?

A recent, colour, passport-style photo of your head and shoulders on a plain background. You upload it with your online application.

### What photo do I need for a student ID card?

Your university or college sets the rules, but most ask for a passport-style head-and-shoulders photo on a plain background, uploaded before enrolment.

### Can I take an ID photo with my phone?

Yes. Use the rear camera, face a window and ask someone to take it. The tool handles the crop and background.

### Is the ID photo maker free?

Uploading and previewing your photo is free. Downloading the finished ID photo and print sheet costs £7.99.

### Will my ID photo definitely be accepted?

No tool can promise that. The card issuer makes the final decision, but a correctly framed, plain-background photo avoids the most common rejections.
