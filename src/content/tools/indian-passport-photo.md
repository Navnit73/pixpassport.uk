## Official Indian Passport & OCI Photo Guidelines

Whether applying for an Indian Passport renewal, Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) registration, or an e-Visa through VFS Global or Passport Seva Kendra, the photograph must adhere to Indian Government standards:

### Key Indian Specifications
- **Dimensions**: Exactly 51 mm × 51 mm (2 inches × 2 inches), with square 1:1 aspect ratio (600×600 px minimum).
- **Head Size**: From the crown of the head to the bottom of the chin must be between **35 mm and 40 mm** (68% to 78% of vertical image height).
- **Background**: Plain white or light off-white background with no patterns or shadows.
- **Eyeglasses**: Prescribed eyeglasses are permitted only if eyes are completely clear with zero flash glare or dark tinted lenses.
- **Attire**: Dark clothing is recommended to provide clear contrast against the light white background. Uniforms are prohibited except religious attire.

## What to Do vs What to Avoid

| Required Checklist ✅ | Common Rejection Reasons ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Square 51×51 mm format (600×600 px) | 35×45 mm UK rectangular size (often rejected by VFS) |
| Plain light white background | Coloured or grey background |
| Head size 35–40 mm (68–78%) | Head too small or zoomed in too close |
| Frontal face with neutral expression | Head tilted or sideways pose |
| Digital photo under 3 MB, high resolution | Low quality or dark phone picture |
