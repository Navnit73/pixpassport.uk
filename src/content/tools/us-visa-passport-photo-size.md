A US visa photo must be **square: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) for a print, or between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels for a digital upload** such as the DS-160 form. It needs a plain white or off-white background, a neutral expression, no glasses, and must be taken in the last 6 months.

This is different from a standard UK passport photo (35 × 45 mm), so a UK photo booth strip is the wrong size for a US visa.

## US Visa Photo Requirements at a Glance

| Requirement                     | US visa photo                                                                                      |
| :------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Print size**                  | 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), square                                                                  |
| **Digital size**                | 600 × 600 px minimum, 1200 × 1200 px maximum, square                                               |
| **File format**                 | JPEG, in colour (sRGB)                                                                             |
| **File size**                   | 240 KB or less                                                                                     |
| **Head height (paper photo)**   | 1 to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm), top of hair to bottom of chin                                           |

| **Age of photo**                | Taken within the last 6 months                                                                     |
| **Expression**                  | Neutral or a natural smile, facing the camera, both eyes open                                      |
| **Glasses**                     | Not allowed                                                                                        |

### US Visa Photo Size in Pixels (DS-160 Upload)

For an online application, the image must be square, at least **600 × 600 px** and no more than **1200 × 1200 px**. It must be a colour JPEG, 240 KB or smaller. If your file is too big, compress it, keeping the compression ratio at 20:1 or lower.

### Scanning an Existing Photo

If you scan a printed photo, scan it at **300 pixels per inch**. A 2-inch photo scanned at 300 ppi gives exactly 600 × 600 px.

## US Visa Photo Guidelines

### What Your US Visa Photo Must Show

- your full head, from the top of your hair to the bottom of your chin
- your head centred in the frame and facing the camera
- a neutral expression or a natural smile, with both eyes open
- even lighting, natural skin tone and a sharp, in-focus image
- clothing you would wear every day

### What Your US Visa Photo Must Not Have

- glasses (no longer allowed in visa photos)
- shadows on your face or on the background
- filters, retouching or digital changes
- uniforms or camouflage clothing
- headphones or wireless devices
- another person or object in the picture

### Acceptable Photos: Head Coverings and Medical Exceptions

#### Religious head coverings

You can wear a head covering if you wear it every day for religious reasons. Your full face must be visible, and the covering must not cast shadows.

#### Medical head coverings

A head covering for medical reasons is also allowed, with a signed statement from you. Your full face must still be visible.

#### Hair and jewellery

Hair must not cover your eyes or the edges of your face. Keep jewellery and piercings from covering or reflecting light across your face.

### Babies and Children

Children need the same size photo. Parents should not be visible in the picture, so lay a baby on a plain white sheet and photograph from above. Our [UK baby passport photo guide](/tool/uk-baby-passport-photo) has tips that also work for this.

## Which Photo Do I Need? DS-160, Interview Print or Green Card

### DS-160 Photo Requirements (Nonimmigrant Visa Online Form)

The DS-160 asks you to upload a digital photo that meets the digital rules above. If the upload fails, or if your embassy or consulate asks for one, you may need to bring a printed 2 × 2 inch photo to your interview.

### Do I Need a Printed US Visa Photo for the Interview?

Some US embassies and consulates ask you to bring one printed photo to the interview that meets all the requirements. Check the instructions on your appointment confirmation and the US Embassy website for the location where you are applying.

### Green Card Photo Requirements

Green card and other USCIS photo requirements are published by USCIS and can differ in details such as how recent the photo must be. Check the instructions for your specific form before you submit, and do not assume a visa photo will be accepted.

### DV Lottery Photo Requirements

The Diversity Visa (DV) lottery asks for a digital photo that is exactly **600 × 600 px**, in JPEG format, within the file-size limit. Check the current instructions on the official DV entry page before you upload.

| Filter, retouching or beauty mode   | Use an unedited photo                                                                |
| Smiling widely or eyes closed       | Use a neutral expression or a natural smile with both eyes open                      |

## How to Take a US Visa Photo at Home

1. **Ask someone to take it.** Selfies distort your face and rarely fit the head-size rule.
2. **Stand in front of a plain white or off-white wall,** about half a metre away to avoid shadows.
3. **Put the camera about 1.5 metres away** and zoom in slightly, at eye level.
4. **Light your face from the front,** with the light behind the camera.
5. **Look straight ahead** with a neutral expression or a natural smile, both eyes open, no glasses.
6. **Crop to a square,** with your head filling 50–69% of the height and your eyes between 56% and 69% from the bottom.
7. **Export as a JPEG at 600 × 600 px or larger,** 240 KB or smaller.

## Frequently Asked Questions About US Visa Photos

### What size is a US visa photo?

A US visa photo is 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) for a print, and between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels for a digital upload. It must be square.

### What are the US visa photo requirements?

The photo must be square, in colour, taken within the last 6 months, on a plain white or off-white background, with your full head visible, a neutral expression or natural smile, both eyes open, and no glasses. Head height must be 1 to 1⅜ inches (25–35 mm) on a print.

### What is the US visa photo size in pixels?

Between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels, with equal width and height. The file must be a colour JPEG of 240 KB or less.

### What is the US visa photo size in mm and cm?

51 × 51 mm, or 5.1 × 5.1 cm, which is the same as 2 × 2 inches.

### What is the US visa photo file size limit?

240 KB (kilobytes) or smaller, in JPEG format. If your file is larger, compress it, keeping the compression ratio at 20:1 or lower.

### Can I wear glasses in a US visa photo?

No. Glasses are no longer allowed in US visa photos, so remove them before the photo is taken.

### What background colour does a US visa photo need?

A plain white or off-white background with no shadows or patterns.

### How recent must a US visa photo be?

It must have been taken within the last 6 months and look like you now.

### Can I take my own US visa photo?

Yes. The State Department recommends a professional service but allows you to take the photo yourself, as long as it meets every requirement. The embassy or consulate makes the final decision on acceptance.

### What is the DS-160 photo requirement?

For the DS-160 you upload a square colour JPEG, between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 px, 240 KB or less, that meets the composition rules above. Some embassies also ask for a printed 2 × 2 inch photo at the interview.

### Do I need a printed US visa photo for my interview?

Some embassies and consulates do. Check your appointment instructions and the website of the US embassy or consulate where you apply.

### Is there a free US visa photo tool?

Yes. The US Department of State has a free photo tool that crops an existing image to 600 × 600 px. It only crops, so check the background, head size and lighting yourself or with our [photo checker](/tool/us-visa-passport-photo-size#studio).

### Can I use a UK passport photo for a US visa?

No. A UK passport photo is 35 × 45 mm and portrait-shaped, while a US visa photo must be square (2 × 2 inches or 600–1200 px). The head size rule is also different.

### Where can I get a US visa photo near me in the UK?

Use a professional photo studio or lab and ask specifically for a US visa photo, 2 × 2 inches, with a digital copy. Confirm the size before you pay, because many UK shops default to UK passport size.

### Are green card and DV lottery photos the same as a US visa photo?

They are similar in size and composition, but the rules for how recent the photo must be and the file requirements can differ. Follow the instructions for your specific form: USCIS for green card applications, and the official DV entry page for the lottery.

### Will my US visa photo definitely be accepted?

No tool can promise that. The US embassy or consulate makes the final decision, but checking your photo with our [passport photo checker](/tool/us-visa-passport-photo-size#studio) first helps you avoid the most common mistakes.
