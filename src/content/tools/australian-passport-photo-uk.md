## Australian Passport Photo Rules at a Glance

If you are an Australian citizen in the UK, you apply through the Australian High Commission in London, and you need **2 printed photos that meet Australian rules**. These are different from UK passport photos, so a standard UK booth strip will not work.

| Requirement | Australian passport photo |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Photo size** | 35–40 mm wide by 45–50 mm high |
| **Face height (chin to crown)** | 32–36 mm |
| **Number of photos** | 2 |
| **Age of photo** | Less than 6 months old |
| **Background** | Plain white or light, contrasting with your face |
| **Print type** | Colour, dye sublimation (not inkjet), glossy, heavy-weight paper of at least 200 gsm |
| **Expression** | Neutral, eyes open, mouth closed (over 3 years old) |
| **Glasses** | Not allowed, unless you cannot remove them for medical reasons |

## Australian Passport Photo Size: mm, cm, Inches and Pixels

The Australian Passport Office sets photo size in millimetres for a printed photo. Here is the same range in other units.

| Measurement | Millimetres | Centimetres | Inches |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Width** | 35–40 mm | 3.5–4.0 cm | 1.38–1.57 in |
| **Height** | 45–50 mm | 4.5–5.0 cm | 1.77–1.97 in |
| **Face height, chin to crown** | 32–36 mm | 3.2–3.6 cm | 1.26–1.42 in |

**What about pixels?** The Passport Office does not publish a pixel size, because it asks for printed photos. If you need a digital file to print from, a 35 × 45 mm photo is about 827 × 1063 px at 600 dpi (or 413 × 531 px at 300 dpi). Use a high resolution, because low-resolution prints are a common reason for rejection.

## Australian vs UK Passport Photo Size

The two sizes are close but not identical, and the head size rule is different. That is why a UK photo can fail an Australian application.

| | Australian passport photo | UK printed passport photo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Size | 35–40 mm wide × 45–50 mm high | 35 mm wide × 45 mm high |
| Head size | Chin to crown 32–36 mm | Head 29–34 mm |
| Background | Plain white or light | Plain cream or light grey |
| Number needed | 2 | 2 for a paper application |
| Where it is accepted | Australian High Commission, London | HM Passport Office |

## Can I Use a UK Photo Booth for an Australian Passport?

No. The Australian High Commission in London says it cannot accept photos taken at UK photo booths, because the print resolution is not high enough. It also asks that photos are:

- glossy and printed at the highest resolution
- not printed on an inkjet printer (dye sublimation is acceptable)
- taken with studio lighting, so there are no shadows behind your head or ears and the light across your face is even

Ask your photographer for an **Australian passport photo**, not a UK one, and tell them the size and face height from the table above.

## Australian Passport Photo Rules in Detail

### What Your Photo Must Show

Your photo must have:

- a clear, in-focus image with no marks or red eye
- a plain white or light background that contrasts with your face
- even lighting with natural skin tone
- your face centred and looking straight at the camera
- the edges of your face clearly visible

### What Your Photo Must Not Have

Your photo must not have:

- retouching, such as removing moles, wrinkles or scars
- shadows or reflections on your face or the background
- your head tilted in any direction
- hair covering the edges of your face
- another person or object visible

### Glasses, Head Coverings and Jewellery

- **Glasses:** not allowed unless you cannot remove them for medical reasons. Poor eyesight alone is not accepted as a reason. If you must wear them, the frames must not cover your eyes and there must be no lens reflection.
- **Religious head coverings:** allowed if you usually wear one. It must be plain, with no pattern at all, and must show your whole face from chin to forehead, plus both edges of your face.
- **Jewellery and piercings:** must not cover any part of your face, especially around the eyes, nose and mouth, and must not reflect light.
- **Hearing aids:** can be worn by adults, children and infants.

### Babies and Children

Children under 3 may have their mouth open, but the other rules still apply and no parent or toy should be visible. For tips on photographing very young children, see our [UK baby passport photo guide](/tool/uk-baby-passport-photo).

### Medical Exemptions

If a medical condition means you cannot meet a photo rule, you must provide either a medical certificate or a completed B-11 form explaining why.

## How to Apply for an Australian Passport From the UK

1. **Choose your route.** Some adult renewals can be sent by post to the High Commission. Other applications, including first adult passports and lost or stolen replacements, are lodged in person by appointment only. There is no walk-in service.
2. **Complete the application** through the Australian Passport Office portal, then print it. Applications cannot currently be submitted electronically.
3. **Get your photos** to the Australian specification (see below).
4. **Gather your documents** and attend your appointment at the Passport Office, Australian High Commission, Strand, London WC2B 4LA, or post your renewal.
5. **Pay in Australian dollars.** The High Commission takes payment in AUD only.

For new applications lodged in person, one of your two photos must be endorsed by your guarantor, so do not write on or mark the photos yourself.

If your photos are rejected, the High Commission will ask for new physical photos. If they are rejected later in the process, it may request digital JPEG files instead, so ask your photographer for a high-quality JPEG when you get your prints.

## How to Get Australian Passport Photos in the UK

You have three realistic options.

| Option | What to do | Good to know |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Professional photo studio or lab** | Ask for Australian-size passport photos, dye-sublimation prints on glossy paper | The Australian Passport Office recommends a professional provider, and it is the safest route |
| **Take the photo yourself, then print** | Take a well-lit photo with a helper, check it, then take the file to a lab that prints on dye sublimation | Do not print on a home inkjet. Check the file first |
| **UK photo booth** | Not recommended | Rejected by the London High Commission because of low resolution |

The Australian Passport Office also advises against online passport photo services and mobile apps, so treat any tool, including ours, as a way to **check and prepare** your photo and not as a guarantee. The final decision is always made by the Australian Passport Office.

## Common Australian Passport Photo Mistakes

| Problem ❌ | Fix ✅ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Using a UK booth strip | Get Australian-size, dye-sublimation prints from a studio or lab |
| Head too big or too small | Keep chin-to-crown between 32 and 36 mm |
| Smiling or open mouth | Keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed |
| Shadows behind your head or ears | Stand about half a metre from the wall and light your face evenly from the front |
| Dark or patterned background | Use a plain white or light wall |
| Glasses on | Take them off unless you have a medical reason |
| Hair across your eyes or face edges | Pin hair back so the edges of your face are visible |
| Retouched or filtered photo | Use an unedited photo |
| Photo older than 6 months | Use a photo taken in the last 6 months |
| Inkjet print or thin paper | Use glossy dye-sublimation prints of at least 200 gsm |

## How to Take an Australian Passport Photo at Home

- **Ask someone to take it.** The Passport Office recommends a photographer, not a selfie.
- **Use a plain white or light background,** and stand about half a metre in front of it.
- **Put the camera about 1.5 metres away** and zoom in slightly rather than standing close, which distorts your face.
- **Light your face from the front,** with the light behind the camera, to avoid shadows.
- **Look straight at the camera** with a neutral expression, mouth closed and head level.
- **Keep your whole face and shoulders in frame** with some space around the edge.

## Australian Visa and Citizenship Photos

This page covers Australian **passport** photos. Photos for an Australian visa or for citizenship are set by the Department of Home Affairs, and the rules and file requirements can differ from the passport rules above. Check the photo instructions in your visa or citizenship application before you submit, and do not assume a passport photo will be accepted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the Australian passport photo rules?

Your photo must be 35–40 mm wide and 45–50 mm high, with your face measuring 32–36 mm from chin to crown. It must be less than 6 months old, colour, in focus, on a plain white or light background, with a neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed, no glasses and no shadows. You need 2 photos.

### What size is an Australian passport photo?

35–40 mm wide by 45–50 mm high (3.5–4.0 cm by 4.5–5.0 cm, or about 1.38–1.57 by 1.77–1.97 inches).

### What is the Australian passport photo size in cm and inches?

In centimetres, 3.5–4.0 cm wide by 4.5–5.0 cm high. In inches, 1.38–1.57 in wide by 1.77–1.97 in high. Face height from chin to crown is 3.2–3.6 cm, or 1.26–1.42 in.

### What is the Australian passport photo size in pixels?

There is no official pixel size, because the Passport Office asks for printed photos. For a 35 × 45 mm photo, use roughly 827 × 1063 px at 600 dpi if you need a file to print from.

### Can I use a UK photo booth for an Australian passport photo?

No. The Australian High Commission in London says it cannot accept UK photo booth photos because the resolution is too low. Use a studio or lab that can print Australian-size photos on glossy dye-sublimation paper.

### Where can I get Australian passport photos in London or the UK?

Use a professional photographer or photo lab and tell them you need Australian-size passport photos. The High Commission does not endorse particular providers, so check the specifications with your photographer before you pay.

### Are Australian and UK passport photos the same size?

No. The UK printed photo is 35 × 45 mm with a head height of 29–34 mm. Australia allows 35–40 × 45–50 mm and requires a face height of 32–36 mm, so a UK photo can fail an Australian application.

### Can I take my own Australian passport photo with my phone?

You can take the photo yourself with a helper, but you still need good-quality prints that meet the print rules. The Australian Passport Office recommends a professional provider and advises against online photo services and mobile apps. Check your photo carefully before printing.

### How many photos do I need and how recent must they be?

You need 2 photos, taken less than 6 months before you apply. For new applications lodged in person, one photo is endorsed by your guarantor.

### Can I wear glasses or a head covering in an Australian passport photo?

Glasses are not allowed unless you cannot remove them for medical reasons, and poor eyesight alone does not count. A plain religious head covering is allowed if your whole face from chin to forehead and both face edges are visible.

### Are Australian visa or citizenship photos the same as passport photos?

Not necessarily. Visa and citizenship photos follow Department of Home Affairs instructions, which may differ from passport rules. Check the requirements shown in your own application.
