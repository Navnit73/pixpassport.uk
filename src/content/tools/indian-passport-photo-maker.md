## Tips for a Good Source Photo

A clear picture gives the cleanest result when you convert an image to Indian passport size.

- **Ask someone to take it.** Selfies distort your face, so use the rear camera.
- **Face a window.** Soft daylight removes shadows from your face.
- **Stand about one metre from the camera.** Keep the lens at eye level.
- **Stand in front of a plain wall.** Our tool fixes the background, but a simple wall makes the cut-out sharper.
- **Keep your shoulders in the frame.** The editor needs room around your head to crop correctly.
- **Look straight ahead.** Relax your face, close your mouth and keep your head level.
