**Quick answer: what makes a UK passport photo pass?**

- Taken in the last month, in colour, with no filters or retouching.
- You alone in the frame, looking straight at the camera with a plain expression, mouth closed and eyes open.
- A plain, light-coloured background with no shadows on your face or behind you.
- Digital: at least 600 × 750 px and 50KB to 10MB. Printed: 35 × 45 mm, with your head 29 mm to 34 mm from crown to chin.

## How to Check Your Passport Photo Online in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Upload or take your photo

Pick a photo from your camera roll or take a new one. GOV.UK says your photo must be a good likeness taken in the last month, so use a fresh picture. Ask someone else to take it if you can: it is much easier to keep your head level and the lighting even.

#### Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG and WebP

Upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image from your phone, tablet or computer (up to 20 MB). If your phone saves HEIC files, most mobile browsers convert them automatically, or switch your camera to JPEG. Always upload the **original, full-size photo**, not a screenshot or a copy shrunk by a messaging app.

#### How to take a photo that passes first time

- **Use the rear camera** and hold the phone steady at eye level.
- **Face a window** for soft, even daylight. Avoid direct sun and flash.
- **Stand about half a metre to a metre from the wall** so you do not cast a shadow behind you.
- **Pick a plain light wall or sheet.** Light grey or cream often works better than bright white.
- **Keep hair off your face and glasses off if you can.**
- **Wear something that contrasts with the background.**

### Step 2: Choose digital or paper application rules

- **Digital application (online on GOV.UK):** a photo showing your head, shoulders and upper body, at least 600 × 750 px and 50KB to 10MB. Do not crop it tightly: GOV.UK frames it for you.
- **Paper application:** 2 identical printed photos, **35 mm wide by 45 mm high**, with your head 29–34 mm from crown to chin, on plain white photographic paper with no border.

### Step 3: See your result and download your photo

Every rule gets a **Pass**, **Warning**, **Fail**, **Check yourself** or **Not measured** result, with a short fix for anything that needs work. When your photo passes, choose a download:

- **Digital photo:** a high-resolution JPEG that meets the GOV.UK digital requirements, keeping your head, shoulders and upper body for upload.
- **Printable 6×4″ sheet:** a 300 DPI sheet of identical 35 × 45 mm photos with cutting guides, ready to print at home or at a pharmacy.

## Why UK Passport Photos Fail the Check (and How to Fix Each Problem)

GOV.UK warns that your application will be delayed if your photo does not meet the rules. These are the failures we see most often:

| Problem | Why it fails | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Photo is older than a month | GOV.UK needs a recent likeness | Take a new picture |
| Image is smaller than 600 × 750 px | Too few pixels for the digital service | Upload the original photo, not a shrunk copy |
| File is under 50KB or over 10MB | Outside the allowed file size | Upload the original file and avoid heavy compression |
| You cropped the photo too tightly | Digital photos need head, shoulders and upper body | Upload the full picture and let GOV.UK frame it |
| Shadows on your face or the wall | Uneven light hides facial features | Face a window and stand away from the wall |
| Background is patterned, dark or cluttered | Your face needs clear contrast against a plain surface | Use a plain, light-coloured background |
| Pure white wall causing glare | Bright white can wash out your face | Use a light grey or light cream surface instead |
| Glare on glasses | Reflections can hide your eyes | Remove your glasses if you can |
| Hair over your eyes | Both eyes must be clearly visible | Move hair away from your face |
| Smiling or mouth open | A plain expression is required | Relax your face and keep your mouth closed |
| Red-eye | Usually caused by flash | Retake in better light without flash |
| Filters or retouching | Photos must not be altered by software | Use the untouched original photo |

### Is the UK passport photo checker online free?

Yes. You can upload your photo and see your result for every rule for free, as many times as you like. You only pay £7.99 if you download the corrected digital photo or printable sheet.

No. We are an independent service, not affiliated with GOV.UK or HM Passport Office. We test your photo against the published rules before you apply. GOV.UK runs its own check during your application, and HM Passport Office makes the final decision.

### Is my photo uploaded when I use the checker?

### How accurate is an online passport photo checker?

It can measure what is visible: pixel size, file size, head size, framing, expression, background and shadows. It cannot tell whether the photo is recent, and no tool can promise approval. A pass means your photo follows the measurable rules.

### What does the checker test?

Up to 17 rules: file format, file size, resolution, shape, one face, head size, eye height, space above the head and below the chin, head tilt, facing the camera, eyes open, mouth closed, neutral expression, background colour, background shadows and a plain background.

### Can I check my passport photo online on my phone?

Yes. It works in your mobile browser, so you can take or select a photo and get your result straight away. There is no app to install.

### Can I take a selfie for my passport photo?

GOV.UK lets you take your own digital photo, but a selfie makes it harder to keep your head level and the background plain. Ask someone to help, or prop your phone at eye level and use the timer.

### Can I smile in my passport photo?

No. Keep a plain expression with your mouth closed and your eyes open. Under 1s do not need their eyes open, and under 6s do not need a plain expression.

### How old can my passport photo be?

It must have been taken in the last month, and you need a new photo each time you apply, even if your appearance has not changed.

### What should I do if my photo fails the check?

Read the fix for each failed rule and take a new picture, ideally with someone helping. If only the crop or background fails, use "Fix my photo automatically" to crop it to 35 × 45 mm and replace the background. For more help, see [how to take a passport photo at home](/how-to/take-a-passport-photo-at-home).

### What size should my passport photo be?

Printed photos must be 35 mm wide by 45 mm high, with your head 29–34 mm from crown to chin. Digital photos must be at least 600 × 750 pixels and 50KB to 10MB.

### What colour should the background be?

Plain and light-coloured, with no texture, pattern or objects. Printed photos need plain cream or light grey. Bright white can wash out your face, so light grey or cream often works better.

### Can I check a baby's passport photo?

Yes. The checker tests the same size and background rules. Babies under 1 do not need open eyes, so treat an "eyes open" warning as expected. See our [UK baby passport photo](/tool/uk-baby-passport-photo) page.
