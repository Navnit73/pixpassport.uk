## 1. How to Check Your Passport Photo Online

Checking your passport photo online is simple. Follow the three steps below, and keep retaking the picture until every check passes.

### 1.1 Step 1: Upload or Take Your Photo

Pick a photo from your camera roll or take a new one. GOV.UK says your photo must be a good likeness taken in the last month, so use a fresh picture. You also need a new photo every time you apply for a new passport.

For the best result, ask a friend or family member to take the photo for you. Someone else holding the camera makes it much easier to keep your head level, your face centred and the lighting even.

#### 1.1.1 Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG and WebP

Upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image straight from your smartphone, tablet or computer (up to 20 MB). If your phone saves photos in HEIC format, most modern mobile browsers convert them automatically, or you can switch your camera to JPEG before uploading. Always upload the **original, full-size photo**, not a screenshot or a copy that has been shrunk or compressed by a messaging app.

### 1.2 Step 2: Choose Digital or Paper Application Rules

Digital and printed passport applications use different photo rules, and our **digital passport photo checker** tests against the right set for your route:

- **Digital application (online on GOV.UK):** Use a digital photo that shows your head, shoulders and upper body. GOV.UK's online service frames the photo for you, so do not crop it tightly yourself. The file should be at least 600 × 750 pixels and between 50KB and 10MB, in colour, and not edited or filtered.
- **Paper application (printed form):** Use 2 identical printed photos measuring **35 mm wide by 45 mm high**, with your head (crown to chin) between 29 mm and 34 mm high, printed on plain white photographic paper with no border.

### 1.3 Step 3: See Your Result and Download Your Photo

You get a clear pass or fail for each rule, with a short fix for anything that fails. Once your photo passes, choose the download that matches your application:

#### 1.3.1 Digital photo file and printable sheet options

- **Digital photo (online applications):** A high-resolution JPEG that meets the GOV.UK digital size requirements (at least 600 × 750 px), keeping your head, shoulders and upper-body framing for upload to the official service.

### 2.1 Face, Pose and Expression Checks

GOV.UK sets clear rules for how you must look in your passport picture. The checker tests that:

- Your whole face is visible and you look straight at the camera.
- You have a plain expression, with no smile and your mouth closed.
- Your eyes are open and clearly visible.
- No hair falls in front of your eyes.
- Nothing covers your face.
- You are not wearing headwear, unless it is for religious or medical reasons.
- No other people or objects appear in the photo.

#### 2.1.1 Head centred, eyes open and mouth closed

Keep your head level and in the middle of the frame, with your eyes open and your mouth closed. For printed photos, the image of you from the crown of your head to your chin must be between 29 mm and 34 mm high. For digital photos, include your head, shoulders and upper body and let GOV.UK handle the framing.

#### 2.1.2 Glasses glare, hair over eyes and red-eye

- **Glasses:** Do not wear glasses unless you have to. If you must wear them, there must be no reflections or glare, and the frames must not cover your eyes. Sunglasses and tinted lenses are not allowed.
- **Hair:** Move hair away so none falls in front of your eyes.
- **Red-eye:** Your photo must not have red-eye. Retake it in better, even light if it does.

#### 2.1.3 Babies and children

The rules are relaxed for young children. GOV.UK says children under 1 do not need to have their eyes open, and children under 6 do not need a plain expression. Everything else, including the plain background and no shadows, still applies.

### 2.2 Background, Shadow and Image Quality Checks

The checker also reviews the picture itself and what is behind you:

- **Background:** Plain and light-coloured, with no texture, pattern or objects. For printed photos, plain cream or light grey in clear contrast to you.
- **Shadows:** No shadows on your face or on the wall behind you.
- **Quality:** Clear, in focus, in colour and not blurred.
- **Size and file:** At least 600 × 750 px and between 50KB and 10MB for a digital photo.
- **No editing:** No filters, effects or retouching. You cannot "correct" your passport photo.
