## 2. What a British Passport Picture Needs

HM Passport Office sets clear rules for every British passport picture. Check them before you start, because a photo that breaks a rule can delay your application.

| Requirement | UK standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| Print size | 35 × 45 mm |
| Digital size | At least 600 px wide and 750 px high, portrait |
| File format | JPEG, in colour, between 50 KB and 10 MB |
| Head size | About 29–34 mm from chin to crown |
| Background | Plain cream or light grey, with no shadows or patterns |
| Photo age | Taken within the last month |

### 2.1 New Passport Photo Standards

Your photo must be a recent, clear and unaltered likeness. Do not use filters, and do not retouch the image. Take it in even light, with no glare, shadows or red-eye, and submit a colour photo that is sharp and in focus.

#### 2.1.1 Neutral expression, mouth closed

Keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed. Smiling is not allowed in a UK passport style photo, because the system and border officers need a consistent face to match.

#### 2.1.2 Clear view of both eyes

Look straight at the camera with your head level and both eyes open and visible. Keep hair, hands and objects away from your face, and make sure there is no glare from glasses frames or hair across your eyes.

### 2.2 Style and Appearance Rules

- **Take off your glasses.** Glasses are only allowed if you cannot remove them for medical reasons, and you may need to explain why.
- **Wear everyday clothing.** Avoid white or very light tops that blend into the background, and avoid uniforms.
- **Cover your head only for religious or medical reasons.** Your full face, from chin to forehead, must stay visible.
- **Keep your face clear.** Pull hair back from your eyes and face.
- **Avoid headphones and accessories.** Remove earbuds, headsets and large jewellery that could cast shadows.

| Do this ✅ | Avoid this ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use a plain cream or light grey background | Patterned walls, shadows or a white background |
| Look straight at the camera at eye level | Tilting your head up, down or sideways |
| Keep a neutral face with your mouth closed | Smiling, frowning or raised eyebrows |
| Use a recent photo taken in the last month | Submitting an old or heavily edited photo |
| Light your face evenly | Glare, harsh shadows or a dark, grainy image |

## 4. British Passport Picture Maker FAQs

### 4.1 What photo is needed for a new British passport?

A new British passport picture must measure 35 × 45 mm, be taken within the last month, and show your face clearly against a plain cream or light grey background. For an online application, upload a colour JPEG of at least 600×750 px. Keep a neutral expression, look straight at the camera, and take off your glasses.

### 4.2 Can I smile in a British passport picture?

No. Use a neutral expression with your mouth closed and both eyes open. A natural, relaxed face helps the system match you to your passport photo British officials see at the border.

### 4.3 Does my passport picture need a specific background?

Yes. Use a plain cream or light grey background with no shadows, patterns or objects. Our tool replaces your background automatically, so you can take the photo in any room with even light.
