## Do You Need a Photo for a UK Visa?

Usually, no printed photo is needed. UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) collects a photo of your face as part of proving your identity, and the application tells you how. That means a "UK visa photo" from a booth or studio is often money you do not need to spend. Check what your own application asks for before you pay for one.

| How you prove your identity | How your photo is taken |
| :--- | :--- |
| UK Immigration: ID Check app (for eligible documents, such as some EU, EEA, Swiss and BNO passports) | You scan your identity document, scan your face, then take a photo of yourself in the app |
| Biometric appointment (UKVCAS in the UK, or a visa application centre abroad) | Staff take your photo and fingerprints at the appointment |
| UK ETA app | You take a photo of your face in the app, and it tells you if the photo does not meet the requirements |
| Some applicants with reusable fingerprints | UKVI may email you instructions to send an image of your face |

## UK Visa Photo Requirements in the ID Check App

If you take your own photo in the UK Immigration: ID Check app, GOV.UK says you must:

- be in even lighting, against a plain, light-coloured background
- keep your head straight and look directly at the camera
- have a neutral expression, with your eyes open and visible
- include your head, shoulders and upper body (extend your arms, or ask someone to take the photo)

You must not:

- have anything covering your face or eyes
- wear a head covering, unless it is for religious or medical reasons
- have any shadows, objects or people behind you

### How to Set Up Before You Open the App

1. **Find a plain, light wall** with nothing on it, and stand a step away from it.
2. **Face a window in daylight** so your face is evenly lit, and turn off overhead lights.
3. **Take off glasses** if they cause glare or the frames cover your eyes.
4. **Ask someone to hold the phone** at eye level, or extend your arm fully so your shoulders are in frame.
5. **Check the frame is clear** of other people, pictures and furniture.

The app checks the photo as you take it, so you can retake it if it is rejected.

## UK ETA Photo Requirements

An Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) is not a visa, but it also needs a photo of your face. In the UK ETA app, you take the photo with your phone camera. The app tells you if the photo does not meet the requirements, and you can retake it before you submit. The same basics apply: plain light background, even lighting, a neutral expression and nothing covering your face.

### Can I Upload an Existing Photo for a UK ETA?

The app is designed for you to take a live photo of your face. If you apply on the GOV.UK website instead of the app, follow the on-screen instructions for the photo step. Check the [UK ETA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/using-the-uk-eta-app) for the current process.

## UK Visa Biometric Appointment: What to Expect

If your application asks you to attend a biometric appointment, you do not need to bring a photo. Staff take your photo and fingerprints on the spot.

- **In the UK:** appointments are at a UK Visa and Citizenship Application Services (UKVCAS) service point.
- **Outside the UK:** appointments are at a visa application centre, which your application names.

Dress as you normally would, remove hats unless they are worn for religious or medical reasons, and expect to remove glasses for the photo.

### Do I Get a Photo Card After My Visa?

Most people now get an eVisa, an online record of their immigration status, instead of a physical biometric residence permit (BRP). The photo on your eVisa record comes from your biometrics, so you do not supply a separate one. See [GOV.UK eVisa guidance](https://www.gov.uk/evisa) for details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the UK visa photo requirements?

In most cases UKVI takes your photo in the ID Check app, the UK ETA app or at a biometric appointment. If you take it in an app, use a plain light background and even lighting, keep your head straight with a neutral expression and eyes open, and make sure nothing covers your face.

### Do I need a passport photo for a UK visa application?

Usually not. Providing biometrics (fingerprints and a photograph) is a step in the application. Your photo is taken in the UK Immigration: ID Check app or at a biometric appointment, and your application tells you which applies to you.

### Where do I get my photo taken for a UK visa?

If you are asked to attend a biometric appointment, your photo is taken there, at a UKVCAS service point in the UK or a visa application centre abroad. If you use the ID Check app, the app takes it.

### Do I need a photo for a UK ETA?

Yes. You take a photo of your face in the UK ETA app. The app tells you if the photo does not meet the requirements, and you can retake it.

### Can I use my passport photo for a UK visa?

You are not usually asked to provide one. Your photo is captured during the identity step of your application instead.

### What size is a UK visa photo?

There is usually no size to meet, because UKVI captures the photo itself. If a separate form asks for a passport-style photo, the standard UK size is 45 mm high by 35 mm wide.

### Can I wear glasses for a UK visa photo?

Nothing can cover your face or eyes. Follow the instructions in the app or at your appointment, and take glasses off if glare or the frames would hide your eyes.

### Can I wear a hijab or turban in a UK visa photo?

Yes, if it is worn for religious or medical reasons and your face is fully visible. See our [hijab passport photo guide](/tool/hijab-passport-photo) for tips on positioning.

### Why does the ID Check app keep rejecting my photo?

The most common causes are shadows, a busy background, glasses glare or your face not being straight. Face a window, stand in front of a plain light wall and try again.
