### Is a Schengen Visa Photo the Same as a UK Passport Photo?

The outer size is the same, 35 × 45 mm. The head-size range is slightly different: Schengen photos usually ask for about 32–36 mm from chin to crown, while UK passport photos ask for 29–34 mm. A recent UK passport photo often works, but a photo made to the Schengen head size is the safer choice. See our [35×45 mm photo size guide](/tool/photo-size-35x45mm) for the full size breakdown.

### Do I Need a Digital Schengen Visa Photo?

Most UK visa application centres ask for a printed photo and take your fingerprints, and sometimes a live photo, at the appointment. There is no single official pixel size for Schengen visas. If a portal asks you to upload a photo, follow its stated file rules.

### Do British Citizens Need a Schengen Visa Photo?

Usually not for short trips. British passport holders can generally visit the Schengen Area visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. You need a Schengen visa photo if you live in the UK with a passport from a country that needs a visa, or if you are applying for a long-stay or national visa. Check the current rules for your nationality before you travel.

## How to Make a Schengen Visa Photo at Home

1. **Stand in front of a plain light wall,** about half a metre away so your shadow does not fall behind you.
2. **Face a window in daylight** and turn off the flash.
3. **Ask someone to take the photo** at eye level from about 1 to 1.5 metres away. Selfies distort your face.
4. **Keep a neutral expression,** mouth closed, eyes open, glasses off.
5. **Upload it to the maker above.** It crops to 35 × 45 mm, sets the head size and cleans the background.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What size is a Schengen visa photo?

35 mm wide by 45 mm high, the same outer size as a UK passport photo. Your head should measure about 32–36 mm from chin to crown.

### Can I use my UK passport photo for a Schengen visa?

Often, yes, if it is recent and meets the Schengen rules. The head size is slightly different (29–34 mm for UK passports against about 32–36 mm for Schengen), so a photo made to the Schengen range is safer.

### What background colour does a Schengen visa photo need?

A plain, uniform, light background, usually light grey or off-white, with no shadows or patterns. Some consulates state a preferred shade, so check your appointment instructions.

### How many photos do I need for a Schengen visa from the UK?

Usually one or two printed photos. The exact number is in your appointment checklist from VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS or the embassy.

### Do I need a printed photo for my VFS or TLScontact appointment?

Most centres ask for a printed photo, even if they also take a live photo with your fingerprints. Check your appointment confirmation.

### Can I smile in a Schengen visa photo?

No. Keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed and your eyes open.

### Can I wear glasses in my Schengen visa photo?

It is best to remove them. If you must wear them, there must be no glare, no tinted lenses and the frames must not cover your eyes.

### How recent must a Schengen visa photo be?

It must have been taken within the last 6 months and look like you now.
