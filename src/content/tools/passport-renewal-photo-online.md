### Background and Lighting

- **Use a plain cream or light grey background.** White is not accepted.
- **Keep shadows off your face and the wall.** Even lighting matters more than any camera.
- **Keep the frame clear.** Other people, furniture and patterns cause rejections.

### Pose and Expression

- **Face the camera.** Look straight at the lens with your head level.
- **Keep a neutral expression.** Close your mouth and keep your eyes open.
- **Clear your face.** Move hair away from your eyes and eyebrows.

### Glasses, Headwear and Appearance

- **Remove your glasses.** HMPO accepts them only in limited medical cases, and sunglasses or tinted lenses never pass.
- **Wear headwear only for religious or medical reasons.** Show your full face from chin to forehead.
- **Look like yourself today.** A photo that no longer matches your appearance can cause delays.

## Why Renewal Photos Get Rejected

Small mistakes cause most delays, so know what to avoid.

| Accepted ✅ | Rejected ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cream or light grey background | White, dark or patterned background |
| Neutral face, mouth closed | Smiling, laughing or frowning |
| Both eyes open and visible | Hair across the eyes, or glare on lenses |
| Sharp, well-lit colour photo | Blurry, grainy or filtered picture |
| Full head, neck and shoulders | Cropped hair or an extreme close-up |
| Photo taken in the last month | Old photo that no longer looks like you |

## How to Take a Better Source Photo

A clear picture gives the cleanest result, so take a few minutes to set up.

- **Ask someone to help.** Selfies distort your face, so have a friend hold the camera at eye level.
- **Face a window.** Soft daylight removes shadows from your face.
- **Stand about one metre from the camera.** Keep the lens level with your eyes.
- **Use a plain wall.** A simple backdrop gives a sharper background cut-out.
- **Leave space around your head.** The tool needs room to crop correctly.
- **Look straight ahead.** Relax your face and keep your head level.
