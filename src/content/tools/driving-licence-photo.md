## Official DVLA Driving Licence Photo Rules

When applying for a UK provisional driving licence, renewing your 10-year photocard licence, or updating your name/photo with the DVLA online at [gov.uk/renew-driving-licence](https://www.gov.uk/renew-driving-licence), your digital photograph must comply with official standards:

### Key DVLA Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 35 mm wide by 45 mm high (same size as a standard UK passport photo, minimum 600 × 750 pixels).
- **Head Proportion**: 29 mm to 34 mm from crown to chin.
- **Background**: Plain cream, light grey, or off-white background.
- **Pose**: Face directly forward, neutral expression, mouth closed, and eyes open without hair across the face.
- **Eyeglasses**: No sunglasses or tinted lenses. Clear prescribed spectacles are allowed if there is no flash reflection or eye obstruction.

## DVLA Application Checklist

| Accepted by DVLA ✅ | Common DVLA Photo Rejections ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Clear, sharp colour photograph | Black-and-white, filtered, or altered image |
| Plain light background without shadows | Background clutter, trees, or wallpapers |
| Full head, neck, and upper shoulders | Extreme close-up or full body shot |
| High-res digital upload file | Blurry, low-light, or grainy smartphone photo |
